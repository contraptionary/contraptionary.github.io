About this site and our tools

This website belongs to the Contraptionary channels. To publish our own videos we use a small in-house tool, Contraptionary Publisher. It uploads finished videos to our own YouTube, TikTok and Instagram accounts through the platforms' official APIs, after a human has approved each video. It is not offered to the public and only ever accesses our own accounts. Details: Privacy Policy · Terms of Service.
